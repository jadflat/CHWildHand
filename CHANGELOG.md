# Changelog

## 1.3.33

- BLACKJACK on the title plays Blackjack Dash on its own; B returns to the title.
- Smaller code.

## 1.3.32

- Blackjack Dash: an ace drawn on a soft 21 no longer leaves a winning 22.

## 1.3.31

- Blackjack Dash: your cards flip over left to right as they are dealt.
- One save page instead of two.

## 1.3.29 - 1.3.17

- New bonus stage, Blackjack Dash: hit, stand, double (UP) or split (DOWN); bank four hands before the clock runs out
  or three misses. Dealt from a four-deck shoe, ties push.
- Deal and hold-card flip animations; hands lay out without overlap, up to three hands of four cards after splits.
- On-screen button hints.

## 1.3.16

- The shop cursor keeps its position between visits.
- Smaller code.

## 1.3.15

- The MATRX backdrop rains bright green.

## 1.3.13

- Hold LEFT or RIGHT to run along the hand and the shop.

## 1.3.12

- The default highlight is dark red; more HIGHLIGHT and ACCENT colours; black suits can be fixed to pure black.
- Clearer pause menu names.

## 1.3.10 (first public release)

- Original songs only: ambient, dungeon synth and jazz.

## 1.3.9

- Animated backdrops (falling code, embers, rain, fireflies, cards, fog); pause menu BG and BG HOLD.
- Two light card faces. Every theme has red and black suits.
- New upbeat jazz songs.

## 1.3.8

- Card colours: eight themes (three colour-blind presets) plus FACE, RED, BLACK, HILITE and ACCENT. Every mix keeps a
  4.5:1 contrast or better.
- Scrolling pause menu with more line spacing.

## 1.3.6

- Card colour themes and a pause menu row to pick one.
- UP / DOWN move the shop selection.

## 1.3.5

- More gore, faster music on some tracks, louder sound, an option to turn card effects off, less lag moving along the
  hand, saved settings.

## 1.1

- Horror reskin: monster dealers (BEHOLDER, MIMIC, DRAGON, MIND FLAYER, CUBE, LICH), nights and hexes, the crypt
  shop, gore and parallax depth.
- Bonus stage every run and after each hex; five card sizes; new wild cards and combos.
- Songs stream from `WILDHAND.DAT`; pause menu song and EQ choice.

## 1.0

- Six dealers, boss rounds, 24 wild cards with four combos, endless mode and Turbo Arcade.
- Bonus stages after each boss; new sounds; pause menu with MUSIC and EFFECTS.
- Pictures and music moved to `WILDHAND.DAT` on the SD card.

## 0.1

- First release: eight rounds, a shop with eight wild cards, a pause menu with save, CONTINUE and NEW RUN.
